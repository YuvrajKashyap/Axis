# AXIS PROJECT CONTEXT

Axis is a personal alignment and reset tool.

Core loop:
Open -> Reset -> Act

## Product direction
- Premium
- Cinematic
- Sharp
- Minimal
- Space / cosmic / blackhole mood
- Anti-algorithm
- Direct and identity-driven

## Engineering direction
- Ground all work in the real codebase
- Respect the current routes, database, and working logic
- Prefer small safe changes
- Do not invent backend systems
- Do not touch 3D unless explicitly asked

## Design direction
- Avoid generic AI-generated UI
- Avoid obvious templates
- Avoid purple-gradient slop
- Avoid default SaaS dashboard energy
- Explore multiple distinct design directions when asked
- Prioritize hierarchy, typography, spacing, and mood
